FONTLOG for Open Thaana
=======================

This file provides detailed information on the Open Thaana
font software. This information should be distributed along with the
Open Thaana fonts and any derivative works.

Basic Font Information
----------------------

Open Thaana is a Thaana typeface covering the complete
Unicode Thaana block, U+0780 to U+07B1: all 24 Dhivehi consonants, the 14
Arabic-derived letters used in loanwords, the ten fili (vowel marks), sukun
and naa.

The fili are true combining marks with zero advance width, positioned over
their consonant by a GPOS mark-to-base feature, so Dhivehi text composes
correctly in any shaping engine.

Units per em: 1000
Glyph count:  50



Generation method: blend
Reproducible with seed: 2757242952

Open Thaana was produced by blending two earlier Thaana designs, both
drawn for Open Thaana, so the whole of the design is original work.
2 glyph(s) could not be interpolated because the parents draw them
with different numbers of contours. Those were taken whole from the
dominant parent rather than being distorted.
